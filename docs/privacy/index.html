Tool inputs and “My setup” (local storage)

The Patch Decoder, Settings Translator, Bottleneck Finder and “My setup” work entirely in your browser. Patch-note text you paste, ticked checklist items, your chosen bottleneck and your saved platform, GPU class and video memory are stored only in your browser’s local storage on your device, so they are still available on your next visit. This data is never transmitted to us or to third parties. You can delete it with the “Clear” buttons in the tools or by deleting this site’s data in your browser settings. Legal basis: storing this data is necessary to provide the function you request (§ 165(3) Austrian Telecommunications Act 2021; Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Interim statistics window

At the moment Google’s consent message is set up on this site but is not displayed. So that visits are still counted only with your consent, we show a small window of our own (“Stats check”) for as long as this is the case. It appears about three seconds after a guide, tool or overview page has loaded if Google’s consent platform has by then neither reported a decision nor displayed its message, and has not reported that the GDPR does not apply to your visit. It asks only about statistics with Google Analytics 4 (Google Ireland Limited); it is not consent to advertising. It never switches on advertising storage, ad user data or ad personalization – these stay “denied” – and it does not change the advertising or the consent message. “Allow” and “Decline” are equal choices. Until you choose, and if you close the window with “×” or the Escape key, Google Analytics stays off; closing stores nothing, and we ask again on the next page.

If you choose “Allow”, Consent Mode is set to “granted” for analytics storage only and Google Analytics loads once, as described in the next section, with Google signals and ad personalization switched off. It sets the cookies _ga and _ga_… on your device, and data may be transferred to Google LLC in the USA (on the basis of the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework). If you choose “Decline” or later withdraw your consent, Google Analytics is switched off on the page at once, analytics storage is set back to “denied”, and any _ga and _ga_… cookies of this domain are deleted; they are also deleted when a page loads and a stored “Decline” is found.

What is stored: your choice is saved in your browser’s local storage under the key ppg-stats, with a version number, your choice (“yes” or “no”) and the time of the choice. The entry is needed to respect your decision and is not sent to us or to Google. After 12 months, or if we change the window, we ask again; an entry with a date in the future is discarded. If your browser blocks local storage, your choice applies only to the current page.

Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR; for storing and reading information on your device § 165(3) of the Austrian Telecommunications Act 2021, TKG 2021). You can view, change or withdraw your choice at any time via “Privacy settings” at the bottom of every page, including this one; pages without advertising load neither ads nor Google Analytics; the window there shows and changes the stored choice and, on request, opens Google’s consent message (see above). If Google’s consent message reports a decision there, a choice stored in our window is deleted as well.

Google’s consent platform has priority: as soon as it displays its message, only that message counts and our window closes. As soon as it reports a decision, or reports that the GDPR does not apply to your visit, only that counts, our window is no longer shown and a choice stored in it is deleted; visitors to whom the GDPR does not apply are then treated as described in the next section. If Google’s consent platform reports that you have not consented to analytics, the Google Analytics cookies are deleted, even if you never used our window. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, Google Analytics stays off and the window does not appear by itself; via “Privacy settings” it only explains this. Visitors in U.S. states: a U.S. opt-out reported by Google’s consent platform (including a GPP or Global Privacy Control signal) keeps Google Analytics off, whatever was chosen in the window; the window does not sell or share personal information and enables no advertising.
