Three things the menu does not tell you

  • “Balanced” is three different settings.DLSS renders 58% of the output resolution per axis, FSR 58.8%, XeSS 50% – documented by the vendors, compared in the name map.
  • 4K with “Quality” is native 1440p.DLSS and FSR Quality divide each axis by 1.5: a 4K output is rendered at 2560×1440. See every mode.
  • Three runs can fool you 1 time in 10.With three runs per setting, a clean win appears by pure chance in one of ten tests. The test log counts it for you.

How Patch Play Guide works

Documented, not guessed

Numbers on this site come from two places: arithmetic you can check, and documents published by NVIDIA, AMD, Intel, Microsoft, Sony, Nintendo and Valve, linked with the date we read them. What we could not tie to an official source is left out.

No benchmarks of our own

We have no test lab and publish no frame rates we measured. Where a page shows example numbers, it says that they are invented. Where it shows vendor figures, it names the vendor.

Made with AI tools, checked against sources

Pages and tools are written with the help of AI tools, checked against the linked sources and covered by automated tests. AdStern, the publisher, is responsible for them. Details and the correction process are on the About page.

Your system decides

A calculator can tell you what a mode renders; only a test tells you what it does on your hardware. The testing guide and the test log are there for that.
