How Patch Play Guide works

Changes, not headlines

A patch note is only useful if you know what to do with it. We mark every relevant change with a plus, tilde or minus and attach an action: re-test this option, check that mode, expect a shader rebuild. The patch-notes guide explains the system.

Typical, not invented

Where we describe what a setting costs, we say “typical” and use qualitative levels. We do not publish frame-rate percentages we have not measured ourselves, and every guide lists its sources.

Every platform separately

PC players change options; console players choose modes; handheld players balance frame rate against battery. Each guide says which platform it covers, and the tools filter by platform.

Test it yourself

Your system is the only one that matters. The testing guide and the 10-minute re-test routine show how to check any change with numbers you can trust. More on our method and limits.
