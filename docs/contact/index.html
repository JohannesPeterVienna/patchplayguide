Contact & corrections

Reporting an error

If something on a page is wrong or out of date – a menu path that moved after a system update, a feature that changed, a link that no longer works – please tell us the page and the sentence, and if possible the source that shows the current state. Confirmed errors are corrected, the review date changes and the page’s change log records the correction.

What happens with a correction

Each report is checked against the official source it concerns – the platform’s support pages, the vendor’s documentation or the publisher’s patch notes – not against other fan sites. Three things help most: the platform and its system or driver version, the exact wording you see on your screen, and a link to the official page that shows the change. If the report holds, the sentence is rewritten or removed, any calculation that depends on it is redone, and the page’s change log gets a dated entry that says what changed. If the official sources do not settle the question, the page says that the behavior differs between versions instead of guessing. We do not publish your name or email address with a correction.

Suggesting a topic

Requests for new guides are welcome, especially questions about a setting or a console mode that existing guides don’t answer. Please describe the problem the way you would ask a friend – “why does my game look blurry in performance mode?” – and name the platform. A topic becomes a guide when it can be explained from official documentation and checked on your own system with the testing method. We don’t write reviews, rankings, rumors or coverage of unreleased hardware, and we cannot promise a date for a requested guide.

Tool problems

If the Patch Decoder sorts a line clearly wrongly, send us the line (without anything personal). The rules are simple by design, so not every case can be fixed, but recurring patterns help us improve them.

What we cannot help with

We cannot troubleshoot individual PCs or consoles, recommend hardware purchases, or help with account, purchase or warranty issues – those belong with the platform or manufacturer’s support. We do not accept sponsored posts, paid links or review samples.
